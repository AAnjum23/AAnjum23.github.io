02 / RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

UNDERGRADUATE THESISSEPTEMBER 2026

Bank Performance and Economic Growth in South Asia

The Role of Human Capital Development and Governance

Examined whether bank performance is associated with GDP growth across Bangladesh, India, Maldives, Nepal, and Sri Lanka, and whether human capital and governance moderate that relationship. The study combined annual-report data with international indicators to build a panel of 668 bank-year observations for 2017–2024.

Estimated pooled OLS and interaction models, with correlation, VIF, and country-level robustness checks. The direct ROA effect was negative and statistically insignificant; the ROA × human-capital interaction was positive and significant, while the ROA × governance interaction was positive but insignificant. The thesis received an A+.

Antecedents and Consequences of the Default Risk of Banks

Studied determinants of bank default risk using Z-scores, CAR, ROA, and ROE for four Bangladeshi private banks, then examined how default risk related to market valuation (Tobin’s Q), controlling for non-performing loans. Group of five.
